Mailka Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Mailka is an email utility developed by Sayika. It helps users prepare and send emails through an email account they connect.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Sayika prohibits the use of Mailka to create, request, send, or distribute child sexual abuse or exploitation material, or to exploit, endanger, or sexually abuse a child.

Reporting a concern

To report a child safety concern involving Mailka, email sajkartglobal@gmail.com. Include a description of the concern and relevant details. Do not email illegal images or videos.

How reports are handled

Sayika will review reports related to Mailka and take appropriate action where possible. This may include restricting use of the app and contacting relevant authorities when required by law.

App functionality

Mailka is designed to help users send emails to recipients they choose. It is not a social network, chat service, or public content sharing platform.

Contact

Child safety contact: Sayika at sajkartglobal@gmail.com.
